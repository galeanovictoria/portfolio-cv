          own teams couldn't fix that without a developer.

    label: What I did
    labelLevel: h2
    contentSections:
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
        text: >-
          I ran eight interviews with partners across three markets — marketing directors,
          sales analysts, support technicians — and built three personas and journey maps
          for the two scenarios that came up in every conversation: *a client has asked for
          collateral*, and *a reseller needs a manual*.


          The research pointed hard at navigation. Almost every participant said global
          search would change their workflow; the existing collateral filters were barely
          used. So I rebuilt the information architecture around finding rather than
          browsing, prototyped it, and validated the structure over three rounds of
          usability testing before a single screen was finished.


          I built the portal's design system on PaperCut's own product foundations, so the
          portal reads as part of their wider brand rather than a bolt-on — then designed
          the collateral library, order forms and sales databases on top of it.
