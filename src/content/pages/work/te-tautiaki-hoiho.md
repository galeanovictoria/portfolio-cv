          Te Tautiaki Hoiho protect the hoiho — the world's most endangered penguin. Their
          WordPress site had accumulated years of technical debt: donations were unreliable,
          and every routine content change had to go through an external agency. For an NGO
          with limited resources, that dependence cost both money and voice.


          Then the deadline moved. The trust learned they'd be featured on *Seven Sharp*,
          primetime national television. A six-week project had to compress — and design
          couldn't be the thing that held it up.

    labelLevel: h2
    contentSections:
      - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
        text: >-
          The old site was penguin blue-and-yellow — a brand built around one bird. I moved
          it toward greens, beiges and earth tones, which is what the trust actually does:
          habitat. The organisation is about the coastline the hoiho depend on, not just the
          mascot.


          To hit the broadcast date, development started before design was finished. That
          only works if the design is systematic, so I worked in components from the start
          and made the palette shift something that could be applied consistently rather
          than page by page.


          The part I care most about is invisible to visitors. I designed for the trust's
          own staff as a user group — component structures they could reorder, fill and
          publish themselves. When a new donation drive lands, they change it. Not an
          agency, not a developer.
